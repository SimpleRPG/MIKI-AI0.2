MIKI-AI Meaning Understanding Deep Audit Phase 84

Final judgment: NOT YET at a reliable general-conversation level.

What works
- Japanese tokenization pipeline and dictionary fallback exist.
- Dialogue-act classification covers common explicit phrases.
- Semantic frames represent targets, exclusions, conditions, action chains and mutations.
- Answer IR and surface-generation meaning-preservation gates exist.
- Unknown or unresolved meaning normally falls back instead of pretending certainty.

Measured weaknesses
- Intent diagnostic exact match: 7/12 (58.3%). Some labels are adjacent rather than entirely wrong, but the result shows dependence on phrase patterns.
- Complex semantic instructions ready without clarification: 0/10.
- Four short continuity prompts all remained not ready.
- Hybrid checks for "これでどう？", "もっと良くして", "問題ない？", and "既存機能は消さないで実装して" all required fallback.
- Existing anaphora shadow benchmark: 85% topic match and 75% response-text match among unique-resolution test cases.
- Existing conversation learning/reasoning regression failed because a second consistent affirmation did not promote the claim from CANDIDATE to SUPPORTED.

Structural causes
1. Reference resolution chooses a recent history line using a small keyword regex. It does not maintain typed discourse entities, salience, relation roles, or competing referents.
2. Dialogue-act recognition is regex-first and single-label. Mixed requests such as confirmation plus continuation or prohibition plus implementation lose secondary intent.
3. Semantic parsing is tuned toward operational instructions. Everyday implication, paraphrase, irony, unstated premises, preference nuance and broad world knowledge are not demonstrated.
4. Clarification is conservative, which is safe, but it causes many ordinary instructions to remain WAITING_USER.
5. Vocabulary entries and morphology improve segmentation but do not themselves supply compositional word-sense disambiguation or pragmatic understanding.
6. The answer surface can preserve an already-built IR, but good wording does not prove that the incoming user meaning was understood correctly.

Operational recommendation
- Current state is suitable for constrained, explicit, domain-oriented dialogue with fallback.
- Do not certify it as autonomous natural conversation or human-level meaning understanding.
- The next implementation priority should be a typed discourse model, multi-intent semantic representation, ambiguity calibration, compositional negation/scope handling, and turn-level semantic E2E corpus with expected frames and replies.
